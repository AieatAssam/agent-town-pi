The
Accordion Road

Nobody crashes. Nobody closes a lane. One car eases off the gas—and a few seconds later, a car far behind is nearly stopped. The queue travels backward, even while every car points forward. Nudge the controls and watch the gap pass down the line.

Ready. Press Send a brake ripple.

This is a schematic, not a traffic forecast. The red car taps once; the yellow car leads. Try a longer reaction delay, then send another ripple.

1 · A small change
The lead car slows a little. It is still moving.
2 · A late response
The next driver notices the shrinking gap and brakes.
3 · The accordion
Each response arrives later. The slow patch can grow and drift upstream.

Real traffic involves many interacting drivers and conditions; this toy isolates one mechanism, not the whole explanation.
