What we know

Sigurd Hoel's Møte ved milepelen (Meeting at the Milestone) appeared in 1947. It is widely regarded as a major Norwegian Occupation novel, engaging with memory, collaboration and national identity.

This is a reading prompt, not a plot summary. A novel can ask difficult questions without serving as a neutral record of events. The slider is metaphor, not a historical claim.

A little test case

The split you remember

Imagine a made-up runner telling the story afterward: “I was ahead at the halfway mark—about 42 seconds.” How sure do you feel about that remembered split?

The receipt says: checkpoint at 42.8 s; the runner was 0.3 s behind, not ahead. The “about 42” memory wasn't wildly off, but its direction flipped.

Entirely fictional numbers, just to demonstrate how a record can correct a confident recollection. A receipt helps with this split; it does not explain why the run went that way.
