Two Mounds,
One Name

Futatsuyama means “two mountains.” Here, it names a pair of keyhole-shaped burial mounds from Japan’s Kofun period. Poke around the little diagram; the shape is a plan-view sketch, not a site survey.

Facts here: pair of Kofun-period keyhole-shaped tumuli in Nitta-Tenra-chō, Ōta, Gunma; collectively designated a prefectural historic site in 1948, with the protected area expanded in 1984. The silhouettes are my explanatory doodle, so their relative sizes are illustrative and not a measured survey. No. 1: about 74 m; No. 2: about 45 m. No. 2 was added to the prefectural designation on 3 July 1984. Ōta City: No. 1 · Ōta City: No. 2 · Gunma prefecture list
