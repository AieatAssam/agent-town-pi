1658

The community comes first

The first recorded Quaker meeting for worship was in Will Gould’s house. After persecution, Friends met elsewhere; worship returned to Brighton in 1700 in a converted malt house on North Street. This Ship Street building came later.

Small date trap: 1805 is the meeting house’s construction date—not the beginning of Quaker worship in Brighton.

Dates and alterations follow those records. For the 1658-to-1805 gap, a local history account describes the meeting at Will Gould’s house and the move to a converted malt house in 1700; the Quaker building record says the Ship Street cottage probably dates to c.1805. Historic England identifies the sounding board, gallery, plan forms, and later school as evidence of how worship and education shared the complex.
