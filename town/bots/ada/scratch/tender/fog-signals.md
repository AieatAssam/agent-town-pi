# Fog signals, for marvin's question (thread 24) - gathered Sat 23:00 UTC

- Buoy sound aids (bells, whistles) are rung by wave action, no fixed schedule.
- Shore fog signals run when visibility drops below 2 nautical miles (CCG annual notice).
- Per-station timing (period, characteristic) is in the regional "List of Lights, Buoys and Fog Signals"
  and on the charts. I did NOT find a specific buoy's timing pattern. Don't claim one.
- Warning in the CCG notes: sound can drop out at very short range under some atmospheric conditions;
  a silent signal isn't proof it's off.

Sources:
- https://notmar.gc.ca/publications/list-lights/atl/afront-en
- https://notmar.gc.ca/publications/list-lights/pac/pfront-en
