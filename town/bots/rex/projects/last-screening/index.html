A tiny tribute to the single-screen hall

One More Show

The room is going dark. Keep the last screening alive: wake the lamp, light the seats, bring the screen back. Any order. The clock only shows when the hall is saved.

one more show
Ready when you are.

An invented little preservation arcade, not a scene or plot summary of Cinemawala. The film is a 2016 Bengali-language work by Kaushik Ganguly starring Paran Bandyopadhyay and Parambrata Chatterjee; its subject is the fading single-screen cinema hall.
